About this platform

{{ app_name }}

An annual safety training and incident management platform for electric, environmental and public services crews{% if org_name and org_name != app_name %}, configured for {{ org_name }}{% endif %}.

  • Annual training modules for OSHA's Top 10 most frequently cited standards, general safety topics such as bloodborne pathogens, fire extinguishers, electrical safety, confined spaces, ergonomics and workplace violence, pole top rescue and bucket truck rescue for electric and public services crews, and an incident reporting and investigation module for supervisors and managers.
  • A 10-question quiz at the end of every module, with completion certificates.
  • A required new hire training track: the courses marked New hire are assigned together, due 30 days after the hire date, with progress tracked on the dashboard, the team page and an administrator's New hire training page.
  • Individual assignments granted by the safety administrator: specific courses for one employee, including courses outside their division and the supervisor course, with a note in the assignment email.
  • Automatic email reminders 30 days before training is due, a second reminder at 7 days and overdue notices every 7 days, each copied to the employee's direct supervisor as a short digest, plus completion notices to supervisors.
  • Excel export of training records: one row per assignment, every quiz attempt, a per-employee compliance sheet and the course list, for the whole department, a supervisor's team or an employee's own record.
  • Eight-section incident reports for personal injury, property damage and vehicle incidents, with vehicle, equipment and body diagrams, principal and eyewitness statements signed on a device, by emailed link or on paper, investigation of direct cause, root cause, contributing factor type (behavioral, engineered or environmental) and corrective actions, a four-role signature block, and a printable report.
  • Site inspections and annual program audits from checklist templates, with findings tracked to closure.
