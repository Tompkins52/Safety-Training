What happens next

  1. Each assignment is recorded with the reason "Assigned individually" and shows that tag on the employee's My Training page and on their supervisor's team page.
  2. The employee gets the usual reminders {{ config.REMINDER_DAYS_BEFORE_DUE }} and {{ config.SECOND_REMINDER_DAYS_BEFORE_DUE }} days before the due date and every {{ config.OVERDUE_REMINDER_EVERY_DAYS }} days once overdue; their supervisor gets a copy of each.
  3. When the employee passes the quiz, a course that applies to their division renews on the annual cycle. A course outside their division is a one-off and does not renew.
