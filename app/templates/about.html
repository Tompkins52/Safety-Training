About this platform

{{ app_name }}

An annual safety training and incident management platform for {{ org_name }}, covering the Electric Services, Environmental Services and Public Services divisions.

  • Annual training modules for OSHA's Top 10 most frequently cited standards, plus pole top rescue and bucket truck rescue for the Electric Services and Public Services divisions.
  • A 10-question quiz at the end of every module, with completion certificates.
  • Automatic email reminders 30 days before training is due, a second reminder at 7 days, overdue notices, and completion notices to direct supervisors.
  • Incident reporting for personal injury, property damage and vehicle incidents, with investigation of direct cause, root cause, contributing factor type (behavioral, engineered or environmental) and corrective actions, and a printable incident report.

Credits

Version {{ app_version }}. Training content summarizes OSHA standards for awareness-level annual refresher training. It does not replace the text of the standards, the department's written programs and procedures, or hands-on training and drills required by those programs.
