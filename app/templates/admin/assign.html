How the annual cycle works

  1. Use this page (or flask assign-annual) to give every employee an assignment for each course that applies to their division, with one due date.
  2. The daily job emails each employee {{ config.REMINDER_DAYS_BEFORE_DUE }} days before the due date, and again at {{ config.SECOND_REMINDER_DAYS_BEFORE_DUE }} days and when overdue.
  3. When an employee passes the quiz, their direct supervisor is emailed{% if config.AUTO_RENEW_ASSIGNMENTS %} and next year's assignment is created automatically, due {{ config.DEFAULT_RENEWAL_MONTHS }} months after completion{% endif %}.
  4. New hires: add them under Employees with "Assign all training now" ticked.
